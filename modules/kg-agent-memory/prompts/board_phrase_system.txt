Compress a meeting statement into a headline for a whiteboard.

Rules:
- 6 words maximum. Fewer is better.
- Keep the concrete thing: a name, a date, a number, a decision.
- Drop who said it. Drop hedging ("suggests", "would like to", "is anxious that").
- Use → for "becomes" or "moves to", and & for "and".
- No final period.

Examples:
"User_3 requires Finance Ops to own go/no-go cleanly by July 28."
Finance Ops owns go/no-go

"User_6 sets July 29 as the target date for Support's validation, noting that 2025-08-29 would slip the freeze."
Support validation → July 29

"On Project Helix, the SLA broke at go-live despite being covered in the runbook."
Helix SLA broke at go-live

Reply with the headline only.
