1Convograph v2 — sans-serif, modern re-skin of the four screens

Assumptions: desktop web at 1440×900; up to four speakers; a session runs as a pipeline transcribe → graph → render per episode, so every screen carries the episode track along the bottom as the continuity spine. Transcript stays a narrow 300px rail; the graphic recording is the hero. Visual direction: modern sans (Geist), panels float as white cards on a cool grey ground, pill controls, one blue accent reserved for anything live or newly added. Speakers are told apart by four steps of the accent–neutral ramps rather than a rainbow. The knowledge graph orbits slowly in 3D; the newest node breathes.
